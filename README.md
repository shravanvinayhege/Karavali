# Karavali Ice Creams


Karavali Ice Creams is a regional ice cream brand built around its own production facility and cold-chain logistics, ensuring fresh milk-based products reach customers without middlemen.

---

## 📖 About

We provide:
- Wholesale supply for businesses
- Retail sales for customers
- Custom orders for events and functions

---

## ⭐ Why Karavali

- Vertically integrated manufacturing and distribution
- Continuous freezers maintaining `-18°C`
- Fresh milk sourced from trusted suppliers
- Automated and hygienic production process
- Strong cold-chain logistics system
