Why Karavali?

We are a vertically integrated manufacturer and distributor. By controlling our own production facility and cold-chain logistics, we deliver consistently fresh, milk-based products to our regional partners — no middlemen, no compromises.

What we offer?

We offer wholesale options for partners, provide retail pricing for individual customers, take orders for events and functions , and ensure our products can be customized to suit your needs.
